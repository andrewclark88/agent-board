After the shipped terminal V1, prefer a short real-world soak period before
promoting new product surface area. The recommended sequence is:

- use the current Ghostty-title + `agents` workflow in normal work and collect
  real friction;
- if the static board proves too passive, promote a continuously refreshing
  terminal board such as `agents --watch`;
- only after the watch/static board tradeoff is understood should the project
  consider notifications, focus/jump controls, or richer resident surfaces.

This is a prioritization note, not a commitment. Re-evaluate once real usage
shows whether awareness gaps are about refresh cadence, acknowledgements, or
something else entirely.
